General Mobile Router FAQs

Mobile Router FAQ

 
Q1. How do I change my DWR-932 default login password ?

From the main page function banner "System", there is an entry "Admin Settings" where you can adjust your password.

 
Q2. If I forgot my password, how can I restore the DWR-932 Router back to its factory settings?

To reset your Router Management Interface password, you must reset your Router to the factory default settings. Press the RESET button simultaneously for five seconds and the router will be reset to the factory settings. Please notice that: this will change all settings including any modified Wi-Fi settings back to the factory presets.

 
Q3. How do I change the SSID (network name) of my wireless network?

The SSID is the name of the wireless network being broadcast from the unlock DWR-932. If you wish to change this, please log into the router, then select Wi-Fi Setting > Wi-Fi Security Settings > SSID For Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz.

 
Q4. Why is the WLAN (Wireless) speed of my router slower than expected?

Check devices/objects that may block the signal such as other wireless devices (mobile phones for example), any electrical cabling or large structures in the house like brick walls etc. And try to move the router to combat these issues.

 

行動路由器的常見問題

移動路由器常見問題解答

 
Q1. 我該如何更改DWR-932的登入密碼?

請您先登入至DWR-932的設定網頁，至「系統」>「管理者設定」頁面輸入您要使用的名稱和密碼，再按下「套用」即可。

 
Q2. 若忘記登入密碼時該如何處理?

請按壓RESET鍵約5秒再放開，待路由器開機完成後再依手冊重新設定。請注意! 此動作將會把路由器的設定全部清空並回復至出廠預設值
路由器預設資訊:
IP位址 - 192.168.0.1
使用者名稱-「admin」無密碼(空白)

 
Q3. 我要如何更改無線網路的名稱(SSID)或密碼?

請您先登入至DWR-932的設定網頁，至「無線網路設定」>「無線安全設定」頁面輸入您要使用的名稱、安全模式和密碼，再按下「套用」即可。

 
Q4. 為何我的無線網路未達到預期的速度?

請您嘗試依以下的方式來改善:
․將路由器放置在較開放的空間，避免有阻隔物且有無線干擾裝置(如微波爐和無線電話)的附近。
․至「Wi-Fi設定」頁面更改無線頻道以避免附近其他基地台的干擾。
․確認無線裝置(網卡)端是否支援與路由器相同的無線速度。
